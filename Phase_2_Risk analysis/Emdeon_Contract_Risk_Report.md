## 1. Executive Summary & Deal Health
This agreement licenses Anatomical Line Drawings from WebMD to Emdeon for integration into medical software products.
From the perspective of **Emdeon (Client)**, the contract presents **extreme asymmetric liability risks** and severe operational restrictions that heavily favor the licensor.

## 2. Clause-by-Clause Detailed Risk Findings

### 1. IP Ownership & Licensing — 🟠 HIGH RISK
**Source Section:** `1. Content.`

**Impact on Client (Emdeon):**
Emdeon risks losing control over the content and may not be able to modify, adapt, or create derivative works from the content as per the terms of the agreement.

> **Red Flag Contract Language:**
> *"Emdeon is hereby authorized and licensed to use the Content by making such Content available to third parties verbatim or as source material via the Software. Such license is a non-exclusive, non-transferrable and worldwide license and shall include the right to use, reproduce, copy and publish the Content solely in connection with the Software."*

**Recommended Negotiation Fix (Attorney Fallback):**
Emdeon should demand a non-exclusive, non-transferable, and revocable license, and the right to modify, adapt, or create derivative works from the content.

---

### 2. IP Ownership & Licensing — 🔴 CRITICAL RISK
**Source Section:** `2. Intellectual Property.`

**Impact on Client (Emdeon):**
Emdeon risks losing all rights to the underlying content/IP, including the right to use, modify, adapt, or create derivative works from the content. This could severely impact Emdeon's ability to utilize the Content as intended in their business operations.

> **Red Flag Contract Language:**
> *"As between WebMD and Emdeon, WebMD or its suppliers retain all worldwide right, title, and interest in and to the Content. All rights in the Content not expressly granted by WebMD to Emdeon are reserved to WebMD."*

**Recommended Negotiation Fix (Attorney Fallback):**
Emdeon should demand that the clause be modified to include express grants of rights to use, modify, adapt, or create derivative works from the Content, if such rights are necessary for Emdeon's business operations.

---

### 3. Termination — 🟠 HIGH RISK
**Source Section:** `3. Term and Termination.`

**Impact on Client (Emdeon):**
Emdeon risks being unable to terminate the agreement for convenience and may lose the right to remove the Content from the Software upon termination.

> **Red Flag Contract Language:**
> *"In addition to expiration of this Agreement as set forth in Section 3(a) hereof in the event either party fails to perform any of its material obligations hereunder, the other party may terminate this Agreement at its election after thirty (30) days written notice to the party of such breach, if such breach is not cured by the party within such thirty (30) day period."*

**Recommended Negotiation Fix (Attorney Fallback):**
Emdeon should demand the removal of the clause in (b) stating 'the other party may terminate this Agreement at its election after thirty (30) days written notice to the party of such breach, if such breach is not cured by the party within such thirty (30) day period.' and replace it with a clause that allows for termination for convenience without a cure period.

---

### 4. IP Ownership & Licensing — 🟠 HIGH RISK
**Source Section:** `5. Trademark License.`

**Impact on Client (Emdeon):**
Emdeon risks losing the right to use the underlying content/IP as the license is non-exclusive and may be revoked by WebMD at any time, as stated in the clause.

> **Red Flag Contract Language:**
> *"Emdeon is hereby granted a non-exclusive and worldwide right to use WebMD’s trademarks and logos (‘Marks’) for the purpose of identifying the origin of the Content during the term of this Agreement."*

**Recommended Negotiation Fix (Attorney Fallback):**
Emdeon should demand a more exclusive and non-revocable license, such as 'Emdeon is granted an exclusive and non-transferable license to use WebMD’s trademarks and logos for the purpose of identifying the origin of the Content during the term of this Agreement.'

---

### 5. Cap On Liability — 🔴 CRITICAL RISK
**Source Section:** `6. Disclaimer.`

**Impact on Client (Emdeon):**
Emdeon risks being held liable for any costs of cover or indirect, special, incidental, or consequential damages, with the aggregate liability capped at $50, regardless of the actual damages incurred. This clause could leave Emdeon without remedy for significant losses, as the cap is not a penalty and applies despite any failure of essential warranties or remedies.

> **Red Flag Contract Language:**
> *"IN NO EVENT WILL WEBMD OR ITS SUPPLIERS OR LICENSORS BE LIABLE UNDER ANY THEORY OF LIABILITY, HOWEVER ARISING, FOR ANY COSTS OF COVER OR FOR INDIRECT, SPECIAL, INCIDENTAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES OF ANY KIND ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT, OR THE PROVISION OR USE OF CONTENT, EVEN IF WEBMD HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES."*

**Recommended Negotiation Fix (Attorney Fallback):**
Emdeon should demand a removal of the cap on liability or a more reasonable cap amount, such as $100,000, and ensure that the clause does not apply to indirect, consequential, or punitive damages. Emdeon should also request a carveout for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or data breaches.

---

### 6. Anti-Assignment — 🔴 CRITICAL RISK
**Source Section:** `7. Assignment.`

**Impact on Client (Emdeon):**
Emdeon risks losing the ability to assign the agreement to an affiliate, subsidiary, or buyer in an M&A transaction without the other party's prior written consent, which could severely impact its ability to manage its business operations and strategic partnerships.

> **Red Flag Contract Language:**
> *""Neither party shall have the right to sell, assign, transfer or hypothecate this Agreement, or delegate any of its obligations hereunder, voluntarily or by operation of law, without the prior written consent of the other party. Any such purported assignment or delegation without such prior written consent shall be null and void and have no force and effect.""*

**Recommended Negotiation Fix (Attorney Fallback):**
Emdeon should demand that the other party provide prior written consent for any assignment or delegation of obligations under this Agreement, including to affiliates, subsidiaries, or in connection with M&A transactions.

---

## 3. Top Action Items Before Signing
1. **Remove the $50 Liability Cap:** WebMD's cap of $50 against an annual fee of $5,000+ leaves Emdeon with essentially zero legal remedy if the drawings infringe third-party IP or cause clinical errors.
2. **Add Uncapped IP Indemnification:** Require WebMD to indemnify and hold Emdeon harmless from any third-party copyright or trademark infringement claims arising from the Content.
3. **Carve Out M&A from Anti-Assignment:** Add an exception permitting assignment without consent in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.
